## 5 · Booklet — English side, pages 1–12

### Page 1 — Cover

```
A little black box.

SHIVAA BLACK
```

### Page 2 — What is in this box

```
Everything here has one job: to make twenty percent
of your next piece of jewellery worth less than it
would have been.

  01  Your Shivaa Black card          — six months
  02  A 0.500 g silver sheet          — 999 fine, yours
  03  This book                       — twelve pages, no filler
  04  A numbered certificate          — signed by hand

Nothing else. We would rather you keep this box than
fill it.
```

### Page 3 — A note from the family

```
We are a family of goldsmiths from Nagaur. Our shop is
on Main Road in Jayal, and we have been weighing,
melting, setting and selling in that market for long
enough to know one thing:

people do not mind paying for gold. They mind paying
for something they cannot see.

Making charges are that something. So this card takes
twenty percent off them — the craft, not the metal.
The gold you pay for is the gold you take home.

Keep the box. Come back with it.

                              — Shivaa Jewels, Jayal
```

### Page 4 — What Shivaa Black is

```
WHAT IT IS

20 % off the making charges on every piece you buy
from us, for six months.

If a piece has ₹10,000 of making charges, you pay
₹8,000 of making charges. The metal, the stones, the
GST and the delivery are untouched — and we show you
all four lines before you pay.

WHAT IT IS NOT

• Not 20 % off the price of the jewellery.
• Not a loan, a credit card or an EMI.
• Not a discount on gold or silver.
• Not transferable to another person or another mobile number.
• Not a reason to buy something you did not want.

We would rather say this here than argue about it at
the counter.
```

### Page 5 — Your card, read line by line

### Page 6 — Three steps to make it work

```
1  Scan the QR with your camera.
2  Sign in with the mobile number this card was issued to.
   We send a six-digit code. There is no password to forget.
3  Your card appears in the app, with the same number that
   is printed on the plastic in your hand.

It takes about forty seconds. After that, the discount is
automatic at checkout — you never type a code.
```

### Page 8 — The silver in your hand

```
Twenty millimetres across, five hundred milligrams of
999 silver, engraved with our number.

It is thin. Real, but thin — hold it to the light and you
will see through it. That is what half a gram looks like.

Four things you can do with it:

  1  Keep it in the sleeve. Silver darkens in air; the
     strip inside keeps it bright.
  2  Put it in your puja — silver is a blessing in this
     house before it is an asset.
  3  Frame it with the certificate. It is a dated object.
  4  Bring it back to the shop within twelve months and we
     will take it in at that day's silver rate against any
     purchase. It cost us half a gram. It is worth a visit.

And bring the card too — first cleaning is free, for life.
```

### Page 9 — Care

```
Gold does not need much. It needs less than you have been
told.

  — wear perfume and hair oil first, jewellery last
  — take it off before sleeping, swimming, or cleaning
  — store each piece separately; a soft pouch, not a box
    full of pieces touching each other
  — bring it to us once a year. Cleaning and polishing are
    free, and we will tell you honestly if a clasp is tired
    before it fails.

Never heat a piece at home to "check" it. Never use
toothpaste on gold or stones.
```

### Page 10 — Renewal

```
Your card runs for six months. In the last thirty days, the
app puts one button on your card: Renew.

One tap. Free. Your member ID and card number never change,
so the plastic in your purse stays correct — only the date
moves. If it lapses, renewal starts again from the day you
tap it. We do not add months you did not earn.

Your expiry date is printed on the certificate in this box.
We will write to you once, on that date.
```
