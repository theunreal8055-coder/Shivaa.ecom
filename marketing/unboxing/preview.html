01The box

A rigid two-piece box with a magnet close — phone-box proportions, not sweet-box depth. 175 × 95 × 22 mm, black matt wrap, gold foil wordmark, blind emboss, cream interior. Everything below is at print scale; the artwork here is a layout mock, the final will use your vector logo.

Sleeve, front — 176 × 96 mm. The cream patch at lower right is unvarnished paper so the serial No. 0142 / 0500 can be written by hand and not slide.

02The card

ISO card size 85.60 × 53.98 mm. Matt, never gloss. The name is printed, the serial is handwritten on the certificate — machine plus hand is the premium signal.

Front — member since, name, member ID · card number, the privilege, expiry, and one QR to shivaa.in/#/black-card.

Back — the four lines that end every argument at the counter: making charges only, six months, one account, not a credit card.

03The 0.500 g silver sheet

The weight is real because the arithmetic is real: 0.500 g ÷ 10.49 g/cm³ = 0.04766 cm³, spread at 0.12 mm that is 3.97 cm² of face — a 20 × 20 mm square. Engrave the actual weight on the back and print the ±0.02 g tolerance. A customer who weighs it must find what you promised.

Face — wordmark, phone, site. Reverse — 0.500 g · 999. Minimum line 0.15 mm, minimum type 1.2 mm, or the laser fills in. Packed in glassine with a tarnish strip; PVC is banned.

04The booklet

24 pages at 148 × 88 mm, saddle-stitched with two gold threads: twelve pages English, then turn it over — twelve pages Hindi. One object, one story, and nobody feels like the second language. All copy is written in full, both languages.

Page 1 and 2 — the cover is a sentence, not a list. Page 2 answers "what did I just pay for" in four lines.

05What the box must say — and must never say

One decision before the printer starts. Your app has two different 20 % products: Shivaa Black — 20 % off making charges for six months, renewable free, physical card delivered — and a Premium card — 20 % off making charges for life, claimed from the home page. A printed card cannot say both. The recommendation is Shivaa Black, and every line printed "six months · free to renew", because otherwise the plastic in her purse contradicts her own app on day 181 — and because six months is a reason to come back, which "for life" is not.

06Eighteen ideas — the extras you asked for

Inside the box
  1. Hand-numbered certificateNo. 0142 / 0500, signed in ink. The only hand-made object, and the one people photograph.
  2. A "for ___" line on the sleeveTurns stock into a gift without a reprint.
  3. Inside-lid line in foil"Rakh lijiye. Agli baar isi dibbe mein lenge." — the cheapest way to make the box get reused.
  4. Today's silver rate printed on the certificateFill it at packing; it dates the box and teaches "rate roz badalta hai, isliye app".
  5. The renewal date pre-printedShe doesn't count six months, and you have booked the next visit.
  6. A blank gifting card + envelope90 × 60 mm, so she can pass the box on without buying anything.
  7. A printed paper ring-sizerWith the app's #/sizer QR. Costs paise, gets kept.
Around the box
  1. Serial → activationEvery box serial also lands in the app, so you can finally answer "how many boxes became members".

  3. One box, many sleevesDiwali sleeve, wedding sleeve, mela sleeve: ₹40 of print per campaign instead of ₹400 of box.
  4. The counter ritualStaff hand it over closed. The person paying must be the person opening.
  5. The three-day WhatsApp"Aapka card chalu hua? (1) Haan (2) Nahi" — one question, and you learn your activation rate.
  6. Bridal variantMaroon sleeve, a 6-line blessing on page 3, the wedding date engraved instead of the serial. Same tooling.

After the box
  1. Renewal as an eventThe app opens it in the last 30 days. Print the date, send one WhatsApp on it, add a free polish.
  2. The silver return is a counter visitBring it back within 12 months, taken in at the day's rate against a purchase.
  3. Box as warrantyBills live in #/my-bills; the box serial is on her account. She keeps the box because the box is the receipt.
  4. Cost per activated member₹480 a box and 60 of 100 activate = ₹800 a member, cheap. 12 of 100 = ₹4,000, expensive. Count, don't guess.
