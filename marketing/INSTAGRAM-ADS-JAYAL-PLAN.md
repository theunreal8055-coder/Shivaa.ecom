## 2. The strategy in one paragraph

Families near Jayal shop for wedding gold **3–6 weeks before the wedding**. The
wedding muhurat season opens in late November, and **Dhanteras (~5–6 Nov)** is the
biggest gold-buying week of the year. So we spend a small **₹1,500 learning
sprint now** (3 days) to discover which audience and which video wins, pause,
review together, then fire the remaining **₹3,500 in the bridal-shopping +
Dhanteras window (26 Oct – 6 Nov)** with only the winner. Total spend is capped
at ₹5,000 — Meta cannot exceed it.

## 6. WhatsApp reply playbook (this is where sales are won or lost)

1. Reply within **5–15 minutes** during shop hours — ad leads go cold fast.
2. First reply script: *"Namaste ji 🙏 Shivaa Jewels, Jayal. Shadi kab hai? Main
   aapko aaj ke rate ke saath 3–4 bridal designs bhej deta hoon. Aur haan — dukaan
   par aakar FREE design trial kar sakte hain, pehen kar dekhiye."*
3. Send 3–4 design photos + aaj ka rate + the map pin. Ask their name + wedding date.
4. Label the chat **"Instagram Ad Lead"** in WhatsApp Business — this is your ad
   report card.
5. Next day, one polite follow-up. Then stop — no spamming.
6. Calls: the ad carries your number; if calls suit you better, we add a
   **"Call Now"** test ad in the burst phase (~₹300 experiment).

- **No "free gold", lucky draw, or prize words anywhere** — the ₹9-lakh biscuit
  campaign stays parked until the CA/lawyer signs off. Our only hook is the
  free *design trial/consultation*.
- Honest claims only: BIS Hallmark, live daily rate, transparent making charges.
- Never invent weights, purities, or prices in ads — same law as the website.
- Budget ceiling ₹5,000 total. Kill-switch: if any ad set costs **> ₹100 per
  conversation** after ~₹500 spent on it, pause it and we revise the creative —
  we don't let a losing ad eat the month.
