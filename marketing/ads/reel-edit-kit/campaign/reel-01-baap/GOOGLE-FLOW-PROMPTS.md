## 4) CAPTION / LIP-SYNC SPEC (edit stage — zaruri)
- **Captions Hinglish (Roman script)** me hi rakho — upar har scene ki caption line di hui hai, copy-paste karo.
- Font: single bold clean sans (Noto Sans SemiBold Latin / Montserrat SemiBold), ivory-white text,
  gold highlight on key words (hallmark, GST bill, 20%, Rajasthan, 89050 05921), halka dark-maroon
  fade backing taaki har background par padha jaye.
- **Sync rule:** caption line appear ho exactly jab wo line boli jaye (Flow ka native audio hi
  timeline hai); har line ≤ 2 rows; word-pop nahi, clean fade-in/out (120–180ms).
- Lip-sync check: har on-camera scene me mouth movement aur audio match hona chahiye; jo Flow clip
  sync miss kare, wo scene regenerate karo ya VO-scene (Scene 6) jaisa off-camera use karo.
- Music: subtle premium instrumental 10–15% volume; **awaaz 100% crisp aur dumdaar**.
- Cuts: hard cuts + 5% punch-in har cut par; export 1080x1920 H.264 30fps AAC 192k; .srt alag se.
