Round chips on the homepage; a tap opens a full-screen reel player with progress bars, swipe, and sound — a pattern every phone shopper already knows by heart.

Shivaa Films

What it is

A horizontal shelf of the four films like cinema posters — number, title and duration on each card, arrows on desktop, swipe on phone.

Why it works

All four films visible in one glance without a single tap; each plays only while on screen, so the motion is alive but never wasteful.

Watch-outs

Cards pre-load just before they enter the screen, and every film ships at ~1–2 MB after compression — the shelf stays light even on 4G.

Step inside the boutique

Four films, one window. Hover — or tap — to bring one forward.

What it is

A full-width maroon band with the four vertical films playing side by side, like standing at the showroom window after dark.

Why it works

The single most dramatic view of the set — four living portraits of the house at once, and it photographs beautifully in screenshots people share.

Watch-outs

Four films at once is heavy, so: on phones it becomes a swipeable row playing one at a time, and the whole band pauses the moment it leaves the screen.
