Poured, not placed

Gold takes its own shape

The frames above morph forever — no two moments alike.

What it is

Each film lives in a generative blob frame — a gold-gradient border whose organic shape morphs endlessly (each on its own clock), gently bobbing, over a canvas of drifting warm bokeh.

Why it works

It's the visual language of molten gold and luxury skincare ads — soft, alive, impossible to screenshot the same way twice. Feels expensive without shouting.

Watch-outs

All GPU-cheap transforms (morph + bob are pure CSS; bokeh is one light canvas). On phones the row wraps to 2×2 and the bokeh halves. Reduced-motion: everything gently stills.

DRAG TO SPIN · TAP THE FRONT FILM TO OPEN FULL-SCREEN
What it is

A 3D cylinder of the four films with true physics — drag velocity, coasting inertia, magnetic snap to the nearest film. Cards dim and brighten by their angle; the front film plays, the rest wait.

Why it works

It recreates the exact gesture of turning a jewellery display in your hand. The single most physical, satisfying interaction of all twenty ways — identical on thumb and mouse.

Watch-outs

Vertical page scroll stays free (only horizontal drags spin). Reduced-motion gets arrows-only, no coast. One film plays at a time — the lightest of the wild ways.

CHOOSE THE TWO FILMS · DRAG THE SEAM ANYWHERE · VERTICAL SCROLL STAYS FREE
What it is

A draggable split screen — clip-path reveal, gold seam, diamond handle — plus pickers to stage any two of your four films against each other. Both films play; the seam is yours.

Why it works

Compare sliders are the internet's favourite toy, and no jeweller uses one for films. “Classic you vs bridal you” is a decision — and decisions convert.

Watch-outs

The seam clamps so it can never hide a film completely. Vertical scrolling passes straight through on phones; only the horizontal drag moves the seam.

The Kinetic Bands — the page has a pulse

Cinema letterbox bars, then two giant type bands — outlined gold, solid cream, italic serif — rolling opposite ways, and their speed breathes with your scroll: scroll fast, the words race; stop, they glide. The films ride between the bands.

Sits: the full-width statement after the rates strip Works: wheel + touch scroll
SHIVAA.IN · WEAR YOUR HERITAGE
What it is

Fashion-house kinetic typography: two infinite type bands (one left, one right) whose speed is literally driven by your scroll velocity, with the four films staggered between them inside cinema letterboxing.

Why it works

The page feels alive under your finger or wheel — that responsiveness is what separates modern from dated. And big serif gold type is the Shivaa brand voice.

Watch-outs

Bands stop dead when the section is off-screen; reduced-motion shows static type with the films intact. The type is décor — real text stays selectable nowhere near this effect.

DRIFT YOUR HAND THROUGH THE GOLD · TAP A FILM TO OPEN IT
What it is

A real generative particle system — depth-blurred, twinkling gold dust on canvas — with a soft vortex that follows your pointer or thumb and stirs the motes around it. The films float in the field.

Why it works

It's the jewellery shop atmosphere itself: dust motes in a sunbeam over the counter. Zero images, pure code — the lightest “expensive” you can ship.

Watch-outs

Particles halve on phones and everything pauses off-screen or when the tab sleeps. Reduced-motion gets a still, starry field. No two visits render identically — that's the charm.
