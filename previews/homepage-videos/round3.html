Scroll, sideways

The Film Highway — the road runs left

The screen pins itself, and your downward scroll drives the films sideways down a dark highway — ghost chapter names sliding the other way, a progress line marking the road.

Sits: a full-screen act between the category slider and Bestsellers
KEEP SCROLLING — THE ROAD RUNS SIDEWAYS →
What it is

A scroll-jacked horizontal gallery: the four films ride across the screen as you scroll down, giant outlined chapter names parallaxing past in the opposite direction.

Why it works

Side-to-side feels like a journey, and the surprise of “scrolling down moves me sideways” is the kind of moment people show a friend. Works beautifully on phones.

Watch-outs

It holds the scroll for a few swipes — Skip button included, and it collapses to a simple swipeable row on old phones or reduced-motion settings.

Everything begins as one thread of gold

Scroll slowly — the thread draws itself, and the films wake as it passes.

What it is

A scroll-drawn golden line (real SVG, drawing itself stroke by stroke) that weaves left and right through the four films, lighting each one — film, frame and caption — as it arrives.

Why it works

It turns four separate videos into one continuous story, and the metaphor is the product: gold, drawn into form. Calm, premium, unforgettable.

Watch-outs

The quietest of the wild set — it needs scroll room to breathe, so it earns its place as a full act, not a strip. Films play only once lit, saving data.

GLIDE THE LOUPE · TAP A FILM TO OPEN IT FULL-SCREEN
What it is

A real magnifying lens that follows your cursor (or thumb): inside the glass, the film under it plays live at 1.75× — the rest of the wall stays dim and still. Tap to open full-screen.

Why it works

It's the single most on-brand interaction of all fifteen ways — this is literally how your customers inspect gold. Browsing becomes appraising.

Watch-outs

Only one video stream ever plays (the one in the lens); the wall itself is posters. The lens lags a touch on purpose — it feels weighted, like a real tool.

DRAG THE CARD · OR USE THE BUTTONS · THE DECK RESHUFFLES FOREVER
What it is

Tinder-style physics: the card follows your finger with rotation and inertia, stamps itself KEEP or NEXT as you swing it, and flies off with a spring. The deck reshuffles endlessly.

Why it works

It's the most playful thing on any jeweller's site, and play converts — every throw is a decision, and decisions are engagement. A natural fit for campaign seasons.

Watch-outs

Only the top card plays video; the rest are posters (light). A tap — never a drag — opens the film, so throwing never accidentally opens.

What it is

A TV set for the house with four channels, a scanline shimmer and a gold ticker — plus the modern streaming trick: pass it by and the film docks into a corner mini-player that follows you, dismissible with one tap.

Why it works

People keep watching while they shop — the film literally travels with them through the catalogue. Of the wild set, this is the one built for conversions, not just applause.

Watch-outs

The mini-player is small, muted and always closable — no hostage video. It pauses the moment it leaves the screen or the tab sleeps.
