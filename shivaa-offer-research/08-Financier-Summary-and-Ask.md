## 1. What we are launching (confirmed parameters)

A festive-season, high-ticket customer-acquisition campaign on shivaa.in — a gold/silver jewellery platform curating designs from 200+ Mumbai manufacturers (up to 4,00,000 designs, with a Gemini chatbot/style-quiz guide):

- **Hook:** one customer wins **100 g of 24K certified gold** (market value ~₹15 lakh at announcement, varies with the gold price — we will never print a fixed rupee value). Announced at the **campaign launch on 12 September 2026**.
- **Winner announced LIVE on Instagram on 31 December 2026** ("New Year Gold Finale") — the campaign's single fixed public date.
- **Purchaser perk:** orders containing **≥5 g gold AND ≥100 g silver** (~₹1.0–1.1L basket) get festive pricing, gift packaging, hallmark certification and purchase perks.
- **Compliant entry:** a genuine **free, no-purchase entry route** on the website with the same quiz — this is what legally converts the promotion from a private lottery into a lawful contest/sweepstake (standard practice across large Indian brands).
- **Marketing budget: ₹9,00,000** across the campaign window.

## 2. The legal & tax position (what the financier will ask first)

**Risk acknowledged and being engineered out — not ignored:**
- A *purchase-required, pure-chance draw* would read as a **private lottery** (prize + chance + consideration) and an **unfair trade practice** under the Consumer Protection Act 2019 — with criminal-provision exposure (Lotteries Act/IPC 294A) and CCPA penalties (₹10L–₹50L).
- **Our fix (industry-standard):** (1) free no-purchase entry route with the same quiz, (2) a genuine skill element in the entry quiz, (3) full lawyer-drafted Terms & Conditions, (4) audited random draw witnessed by an independent CA firm + live streamed + recording retained, (5) "void where prohibited" with state exclusions (Tamil Nadu, West Bengal; Maharashtra rules reviewed), (6) ads worded as "**win a chance**" — never "100% free gold" — with material conditions stated prominently, (7) ASCI/CCPA-approved copy and Meta's mandatory Instagram disclaimer.

**Tax (CA-briefed):**
- Winner's winnings taxable at a flat **~30% + cess (≈31.2%)** — Section 393(3) Sl. 1 of the new Income-tax Act 2025 (formerly §194B). In-kind prize ⇒ **winner pays the TDS (~₹4.7L) before taking delivery**; Shivaa deducts, deposits and issues Form 16A/26AS. Decision published in the T&C before launch.
- GST: 3% on gold/silver value; the gifted biscuit is treated as a business-promotion expense with **ITC reversal** — CA's written model required before purchase.
- If funds arrive as a **loan**, it will be documented under Companies Act deposit rules (Section 73) so it is never mischaracterised as an unauthorised deposit.

## 5. Top risks & mitigations (for the financier's risk review)

1. **Scheme read as lottery** → free-entry route + skill quiz + written lawyer opinion before any public claim. 
2. **CCPA "100% free" action** → all copy reworded to "win a chance"; conditions prominent; ASCI/CCPA review.
3. **Gold price rises by 31 Dec** → purchase ≤30 Sep or hedge; budget shown as a range.
4. **One bad vendor (fake/under-carat gold) becomes our liability** → seller-of-record vendor agreements with hallmark/HUID warranty + indemnity; batch QC before listing.
5. **Winner is a free entrant who never bought (the NPN trap)** → consolation offers + purchaser perks drive the actual profit; odds disclosed.
6. **Site crash on a viral day** → load testing + queue/rate-limiter before Phase 4 spend.
7. **Draw perceived as rigged** → independent CA-firm witness + full recording retained 8 years.
