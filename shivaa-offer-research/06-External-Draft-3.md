**Executive Summary for Financier**

## 1. Legal Compliances (Consumer Court & Statutory)

This is the most critical aspect. If you do not structure this correctly, you can be sued or fined.

### A. The "Prize Chit and Money Circulation Schemes (Banning) Act, 1978"

- The Issue: You are requiring a purchase to enter a lucky draw. If the prize is funded by the pooled money of the participants, it is a "Chit" and is illegal.
- The Solution: You must structure this as a "Prize Competition" or "Free Entry".
  - Free Entry Route: To avoid gambling laws, you must include a "No Purchase Necessary" (NPN) clause. You must allow people to enter the draw for free by sending a handwritten postcard or filling a form without buying anything.
  - In the Terms & Conditions: Clearly state that the purchase is for the goods only, and the price of the goods is the market price (not inflated to fund the prize).

### B. The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (E-commerce Rules)

- Misleading Advertisement: If you promise "100% free gold," but it's actually a lottery, you are liable for misleading ads. The wording must be "Win a chance to get 100g Gold" or "Lucky winner gets 100g Gold."
- Display Requirements: Your website must display: Country of origin, Seller details (which of the 200 manufacturers is fulfilling it), Return/Refund policy, and Grievance officer details.
- Delivery Liability: Since you are an aggregator, if a manufacturer fails to deliver or sends fake gold, YOU are liable to the consumer. You need a strict vendor agreement with the 200 manufacturers regarding BIS Hallmarking.

### C. The Legal Metrology Act (Packaging Rules)

- Since you are selling physical gold and silver, every piece delivered must have the exact weight, purity (e.g., 916 Hallmark), and manufacturer details on the invoice and packaging.

### D. Goods and Services Tax (GST) – CA Compliance

- E-commerce Operator (ECO) Rules: Since you are aggregating 200 sellers, you must register as an E-commerce Operator (Section 9(5) of CGST Act). You are liable to collect TCS (Tax Collected at Source) at 1% (0.5% CGST + 0.5% SGST) on the net value of goods sold through your portal.
- GST on Gold: Gold attracts 3% GST. Silver attracts 3% GST. You cannot show this as zero-rated.
- Prize Distribution Tax: Under Section 194B of the Income Tax Act, if the prize value exceeds Rs. 10,000, you must deduct TDS at 31.2% (30% + Surcharge + Cess) before handing over the gold. You will have to pay this TDS to the government. You can absorb this cost or deduct it from the prize (e.g., "Winner must bear applicable taxes").

### E. BIS Hallmarking

- Selling gold without a BIS Hallmark is a criminal offense in India. You must ensure all 5g gold coins/biscuits and silver items are BIS Hallmarked. The 100g winning biscuit must be BIS Hallmarked (995 purity or 999).

Phase 2: The Launch (September 12)

Phase 3: The Sustain Phase (Sept 12 – Oct 12)
7. Fulfillment: You will likely get a flood of orders. You must have a dedicated operations team to ensure manufacturers dispatch within 7 days.

Phase 4: The Draw (October 12)
9. Audit the Draw: Hire a third-party auditor (a CA firm) to supervise the computerized random draw. This protects you from "Rigging" accusations. Do this on Instagram Live.

Phase 5: The Final Stretch (Oct 12 – Nov 11)
11. Retention: You now have a massive list of users who didn't win. Run a "Consolation Offer" campaign. "We didn't win the 100g, but here is a special making-charge discount for Bhai Dooj." This is where you actually make your profit.

## 4. Positives and Negatives (To present to Financier)

Positives (Why it will work):
- High Perceived Value: The chance to win 15 Lakhs for a minimum spend of ~₹35k (5g gold) is a massive psychological hook.
- Crowded Market Differentiation: Everyone sells gold. You are selling an experience and a dream.
- Vendor Model: You are not holding inventory. You are just passing orders to the 200 manufacturers. Low asset risk.
- Festive Sentiment: Sept-Nov is peak gold buying season in India.

Negatives / Drawbacks (The Real Risks):
- The "Gambling" Stigma: High-end, educated customers might find the "Lucky Draw" tactic cheap or scammy for a jewellery brand. It might attract bargain hunters, not loyal repeat customers.
- Logistical Nightmare: Managing 200 different manufacturers, their shipping times, and their quality control under one brand name is extremely difficult. If one sends fake gold, Shivaa.in gets sued.
- Cash Flow Crunch: You have to spend ₹5 Lakhs on Ads + ₹15 Lakhs on the prize + TDS upfront. Your margin on 5g gold sales will be very thin. You need massive volume just to break even.
- The "No Purchase Necessary" Trap: Because you legally have to allow free entries (to avoid gambling laws), you might get thousands of free entries, and the winner might not have even purchased anything. You must calculate this risk.
