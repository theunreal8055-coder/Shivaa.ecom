Part II

The Compliance & Advisory Playbook

Legal · CA / Tax · Consumer Court — Do’s & Don’ts from the consumer & advertising lawyer, the CA, the consumer-court specialist, and BIS / metrology / AML / DPDP advisers. Research-based guidance — not a substitute for written opinions.

Compliance 1/5 · Legal — lottery law

Making the draw lawful

✓ DO — MUST

  • Free no-purchase entry, same quiz, equal odds — removes “consideration”
  • Real, scored skill element with published criteria
  • Written lawyer opinion before entries open
  • Full T&C pre-launch: age, IST dates, odds, method, tax, re-draw
  • Exclude TN / WB; review Maharashtra & Rajasthan; “void where prohibited”
  • Sell at genuine market price; nothing is “paid to enter”
  • CA/notary witness; SHA-256 freeze; recording kept 8 years
  • Word as “contest / festive reward” — never “lottery”
  • Publish results promptly on the promotion channels
  • Disclose every odds mechanic — keep free entrants equal

✗ DON’T

  • No purchase-only random draws (prize + chance + consideration)
  • No token quizzes — skill must be real and decisive
  • No “100% free” or fixed rupee-value promises
  • No employees / relatives / vendors / agencies in the draw
  • No hidden exclusions or fine-print-only terms
  • No delayed or selective result publication
  • No winner fees — only statutory TDS may be deducted
  • No chit / deposit / “guaranteed return” language
  • No rigged or self-dealt draws
  • No launch before the written legal opinion (G1)
Compliance 2/5 · CA — income tax & TDS

Prize, tax & GST — engineered

✓ DO — MUST

  • Income-tax Act 2025 §128 (was §115BB): ~31.2% on winnings; TDS §393(3) Sl.1
  • Value the biscuit at draw-date FMV
  • Winner pays ~31.2% before delivery; Shivaa deposits + Form 16A/26AS
  • PAN before release (higher rate without PAN)
  • Quarterly TDS returns on time; reconcile 26AS
  • Gross-up model: ~Rs 15L ÷ 0.688 ≈ Rs 21.8L if absorbed (CA-confirmed)
  • §194R does NOT apply to a retail consumer winner
  • GST 3% gold/silver jewellery (HSN 7113); e-invoice at threshold
  • ITC reversal model for the free biscuit (§17(5)(h)) in writing
  • PMLA/FIU-IND: CTR at cash ≥ Rs 10L; STR on suspicion

✗ DON’T

  • No release before TDS deposit + PAN
  • No flat-30% quoting — effective ~31.2%
  • No wrong TDS section (§194A / §194R)
  • No under-valued FMV to cut tax
  • No missed TDS deadlines
  • No silent gross-up without accounting
  • No guess on surcharge/cess — get the CA’s computation
  • No sales before GSTIN is live
  • No ITC claimed on the gifted biscuit
  • No records deleted — 8-year retention
Compliance 3/5 · Consumer-court specialist

Consumer protection & e-commerce

✓ DO — MUST

  • Display legal name, CIN, GSTIN, address, grievance officer
  • Publish returns/refunds/exchange, delivery, price breakup
  • 48-hr acknowledgement / 30-day redress; log everything
  • Honour the promise exactly — certified 100 g, on the stated terms
  • Evidence file: method, witness, recording, results — 8 years
  • Hallmarked gold (HUID); declared silver purity; invoice weights
  • Vendor warranties + indemnities enforced — seller of record
  • DPDP notice + consent for phone / PAN / quiz data
  • Staffed, visible grievance desk
  • Time-bound responses to CCPA / commission notices

✗ DON’T

  • No “100% free” claims in any material
  • No hidden charges for the winner (TDS only)
  • No draw bias toward purchasers or staff
  • No fine-print-only rules
  • No fake reviews or “Shivaa-made” claims on vendor goods
  • No unhallmarked gold jewellery
  • No weight mismatch vs invoice
  • No ignored consumer notices
  • No indefinite personal-data retention
  • No denying liability by blaming the manufacturer
Compliance 4/5 · Product, platform & data

BIS · Meta · AI · DPDP

✓ DO — MUST

  • BIS + HUID on every gold jewellery SKU (mandatory)
  • Silver purity declared (925 / 999, IS 2112:2025)
  • Prize from a certified refiner — certificate + serial + insurance
  • Meta rules: business account, official rules, exact Instagram disclaimer
  • #ad on influencer posts; retain records
  • DPDP: notice + consent, purpose limits, retention, processor agreements
  • Disclose the AI; human escalation; no AI in the draw
  • Legal Metrology labels; watch country-of-origin draft filter
  • Vendor IP licence for photos / designs
  • Breach readiness: notify + report within 72h

✗ DON’T

  • No unhallmarked gold jewellery — ever
  • No mis-stated purity (916 vs 999; biscuit vs jewellery)
  • No “BIS-verified” claim while auto-check is disconnected
  • No personal-account promotions
  • No tag-to-win / share-to-win as the only entry
  • No data use beyond stated purpose
  • No AI promises on prize, purity or tax
  • No unlicensed imagery (Copyright / Designs Act)
  • No orphaned data without deletion path
  • No vendor-KYC shortcuts
Compliance 5/5 · Governance & gate

Oversight & the launch gate

✓ DO — MUST

  • Board resolution: offer, prize, Rs 9L + Rs 1.5 Cr, custodian, draw authority
  • Lawyer + CA briefed before 12 Sep; written opinions before entries
  • Segregate duties: draw, ledger, custody, finance
  • Insure + secure the prize (2-person custody log)
  • Test purchase → quiz → free entry → simulated draw
  • Load-test + queue before Dhanteras / Diwali / finale
  • Vendor agreements with 20–30 makers first
  • Weekly compliance check-ins; re-draw rule published
  • Monthly MIS; campaign report by 31 Jan 2027
  • G1–G10 gate register green before launch

✗ DON’T

  • No launch before written opinions exist
  • No spending the December reserve early
  • No single person controlling draw + ledger + prize
  • No quiet free-route fade
  • No code change near the draw without retest
  • No records deletion (8-year floor)
  • No unannounced mid-campaign rule changes
  • No release without PAN + TDS + acknowledgement
  • No informal loan documentation
  • No sign-off theatre — opinions are the product
