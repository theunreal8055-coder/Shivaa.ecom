## 3. What to keep from this draft (now merged into the pack)

1. **Aggregator liability**: if a manufacturer fails to deliver or ships fake/under-carat gold, *Shivaa is the seller of record and bears the consumer liability* — strict vendor agreements (shipping SLA, BIS/HUID warranty, returns, indemnity) before listing. (Also in file 04 §6, file 01 §5.10.)
2. **Website load + queue testing** before any mass ad spend (a Myntra-sale-style queue/rate-limiter for the entry + checkout flow).
3. **Third-party CA-firm audit of the random draw + Instagram Live + public ceremony with security** for handover.
4. **The NPN "trap"**: because free entries are mandatory and equal-chance (file 05 D2), the winner may have bought nothing — plan the consolation/retention funnel (making-charge discount offers to *purchasers and non-winning entrants*), which is where profit actually comes from. This is a genuinely good strategic catch.
5. **Festive delivery anxiety**: gold buyers panic about delivery; dedicated ops + temporary CS staff with a visible grievance SLA (48-hr ack / 30-day redress) before the draw.
6. **Quiz = simple festival/preference question** feeding the Gemini recommendation engine and cart value (fits the repo's Feature roadmap; keep any *skill* element separate per file 05 D3).
7. **Cash-flow model**: collect upfront, negotiate 15/30/45-day settlement with manufacturers — but this only works in the true marketplace structure (see row 4) or with careful balance-sheet management if Shivaa is seller of record; CA to confirm.
