# Shivaa Inc. — 90 second ANIMATED EXPLAINER · kaise banana hai

**9 clips × 10.00 second = 90.00 second.** Do alag pack:

| Pack | Kiske liye | Files |
|---|---|---|
| `reel-9x16/` | Instagram Reel · YouTube Shorts · WhatsApp Status | clip-01…09.txt (vertical layout) |
| `youtube-16x9/` | YouTube · website · TV screen | clip-01…09.txt (horizontal layout) |

Dono packs mein **content same** hai — sirf composition alag hai (Reel mein graphics upar,
host neeche; YouTube mein graphics left, host right).

---

## Har clip ke saath 2 files attach karni hain

1. **`reference-presenter.jpg`** — host ka chehra lock karne ke liye (wahi aurat, wahi saree)
2. **`logo-shivaa-gold.png`** — bottom-right watermark + outro ke liye (transparent, saaf kiya hua)

`logo-shivaa-white.png` aur `logo-shivaa-navy.png` bhi diye hain agar kabhi light background pe
chahiye ho.

## Order aur QC

1. `clip-01.txt` paste karo + dono images attach → generate.
2. Check karo: **10.00s**, wahi host, **bottom-right pe logo poore 10 second** dikh raha hai,
   English text ki spelling sahi hai, aur graphics saaf hain (bachkane cartoon nahi).
3. Fir clip-02 … clip-09. Har prompt ke **section 9** mein likha hai ki pichhle chapter se
   kya same rehna chahiye — studio, light, music, host ki jagah.
4. Kuch alag lage to **sirf wahi clip** dobara generate karo, prompt ke top pe ye line laga kar:
   `SAME STUDIO, SAME HOST POSITION, SAME MUSIC AS THE PREVIOUS CHAPTER — this is a continuing explainer, not a new video.`
5. Saaton… nahin, **9 ke 9 clips** `01.mp4 … 09.mp4` naam se mujhe bhej do — main jodunga,
   90.00s exact karunga, awaaz level barabar karunga, aur agar kisi clip mein watermark miss ho
   gaya to **main khud post mein logo watermark laga dunga**.

## Pichhli video confusing kyun lagi thi, aur ab kya badla

| Pehle | Ab |
|---|---|
| Cinematic trailer — dekhne mein sundar, par samajh nahi aata kya bech rahe hain | **Explainer** — ek baat, ek graphic, ek chapter |
| B2B aur retail ki baatein mili-juli thi | Saaf do hisse: **FOR JEWELLERS (2–5)** aur **FOR CUSTOMERS (6–8)** |
| Har clip nayi jagah, tootata hua | Ek hi studio, ek hi host position, ek hi music — sirf graphics badalte hain |
| Koi watermark nahi | **Har second bottom-right pe logo** |
| Outro AI ka likha hua | Outro mein **aapka asli logo** — rectangle draw hota hai, script pen-stroke se likha jaata hai, phir shivaa.in |
